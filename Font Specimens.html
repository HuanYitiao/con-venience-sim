License at a glance

  • OFL 1.1 — free commercial use, embedding into firmware and apps, modification, redistribution. The only constraint: can't sell the font itself in isolation. Safe for the whole con-venience pipeline.
  • u8g2 BDF fonts (on-device) — BSD 2-Clause. Already baked into firmware builds.

If pixel-perfection matters

  • Silkscreen / Pixelify Sans are close to the u8g2 shipping glyphs but not identical.
  • For marketing screenshots, extract glyphs from logisoso, inb16, profont BDF sources and ship as a self-hosted woff2 — same license (BSD), 1:1 fidelity.
